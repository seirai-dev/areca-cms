This page shows you where things are in the CMS: how to sign in, how to move between sites, how a content list works, and how to find things quickly. Once you know the layout, the rest of the guide is easy to follow.

## Sign in

Open the sign-in address your agency gave you. Enter your work email and password, then select **Sign in**. Some agencies also let you sign in with a provider such as Google, GitHub, Microsoft or Discord. In that case you see a **Continue with** button for each provider.

If two-factor authentication is on for your account, the sign-in asks for an **Authentication code**. Enter the six-digit code from your authenticator app. If you do not have your phone, select **Use a backup code** and enter one of the backup codes you saved. Each backup code works once.

<Callout>
If you were invited, open the link from the invitation email. It is valid for seven days. If it no longer works, ask the person who invited you for a new one.
</Callout>

To turn on two-factor authentication for your own account, see [Account and security](/docs/cms/account).

## The workspace switcher

At the top of the sidebar you see the name of the site you are working on, with its address and whether it is live. Select it to open **Switch workspace**.

The list shows your **Recent** sites first, followed by all **Sites** you can open. Type in **Search sites…** to find one by its name or slug. A client member can open every site that belongs to the client.

## The sidebar

The sidebar lists the parts of the site you can use:

- **Overview** shows what needs your attention, shortcuts to create entries and recent edits.
- **Content** lists each content type, such as Articles, with its number of entries. Select one to open its list.
- **Library** holds the images and PDFs shared by the whole client. See [Library](/docs/cms/library).
- **People** appears to owners and agency admins. **Settings** appears only to agency admins.

The **Go live** box at the bottom of the sidebar shows the state of the live site. It says whether the site is up to date, has changes ready, is going live, or whether the last release failed. Select it to open the Go live panel. See [Going live](/docs/cms/going-live).

Agency admins also see an **Agency** section with **Overview**, **Clients**, **People**, **Activity**, **Branding** and **AI**.

Select **Collapse sidebar** to make more room for the content. Select **Expand sidebar** to open it again.

## The content list

Select a content type in the sidebar to open its list. The list shows every entry of that type, with its status, when it was last edited and who edited it.

![The Posts list for Harbor & Pine Bakery with entries in several states, such as drafts, ready and live posts](/screenshots/current-collection.png)

Across the top of the list you find these controls:

- **Search**. Select it, or press `/`, to type and filter the list by name.
- **List** and **Cards** views. **Cards** shows each entry as a tile with its image. **List** shows the entries as rows.
- **Select** lets you pick several entries and act on them together. See [Writing and editing entries](/docs/cms/writing-entries) for the bulk actions.
- **New** followed by the content type name, for example **New article**, creates an entry. Press `n` as a shortcut.

## Filters

Under the header, a row of filters shows only the entries with a certain status.

| Filter | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every entry, whatever its status. |
| **Drafts** | Entries that are still drafts. They have never been marked ready, or they were moved back to draft. |
| **Ready & live** | Entries that are marked ready, including those already on the live site. |
| **Scheduled** | Entries with a set time when they will go live. |
| **Archived** | Entries you have archived. They are kept in the CMS but left off the website. |

If a filter shows nothing, the list says **Nothing here has this status yet**. Select **All** to see every entry again.

The list loads 25 entries at a time. Select **Show more** at the bottom to see the next page. The counter shows how many you have seen, for example **Showing 25 of 40**.

Each entry shows a status label. The labels are explained on [Drafts, ready and live](/docs/cms/statuses-and-review).

## Search and the command palette

Press **⌘K** on a Mac, or **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux, to open the command palette. It starts with the text **Search or jump to…**

From here you can jump to a page, find an entry by its title, open a site, create an entry, upload to the Library, change the language, or switch between light and dark appearance. The groups you see depend on your role.

Press `g` and then `o` to go to Overview, `g` and then `l` for the Library, `g` and then `p` for People, and `g` and then `s` to switch the workspace. These shortcuts are not available while you are typing in a field.

To see every shortcut, open your account menu and select **Keyboard shortcuts**.

## Your account menu

Select your name or picture at the bottom of the sidebar to open the account menu. From there you can change the appearance, change the interface language, open your **Account** page and sign out.

Changing the interface language changes only the menus and buttons. It does not translate the text you write in entries.

## Use the CMS on a phone

On a phone, the layout changes to suit a small screen.

- The top bar shows the workspace control, a search button, the Go live status and your account picture. The Go live status shows **Not live**, **Going live**, **Failed** or the number of changes ready.
- The bottom tabs take you to **Overview**, **Content**, **Library** and **More**.
- **Content** lists the content types of the site. Select one to open its list.
- **More** holds the pages your role allows, such as **People**, **Settings**, **Activity** and **Go live**.

![The Posts list for Harbor & Pine Bakery on a phone-sized screen, with the bottom tabs](/screenshots/current-mobile-collection.png)

The editor works the same way on a phone. Your drafts still save automatically. Open **Details** to change the slug, fields, SEO settings and history.
