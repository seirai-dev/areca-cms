title: History and activity
description: Restore an earlier version of an entry from its history, and review who did what across a site with the activity log.
---

Areca keeps two records of past changes. **History** is the list of saves and status changes for a single entry, and it lets you go back to an earlier version. **Activity** is the log of actions across a site or client, such as entries marked ready and releases going live.

## Entry history

Every entry keeps its history. Open an entry, expand **Details**, and find the **History** section. The history starts with the first save.

Each item shows one change. These are the kinds of items you will see:

| Item | When it appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Created** | The entry was first created. |
| **Edited** | The content was saved. |
| **Marked as ready** | An owner marked the entry ready. |
| **Scheduled** | The entry was scheduled to go live at a set time. |
| **Schedule cancelled** | The entry's schedule was cancelled. |
| **Moved back to draft** | The entry was no longer ready. |
| **Archived** | The entry was archived. |
| **Restored an earlier version** | A previous version was restored into the draft. |

Areca saves a revision each time the entry is saved, which can be often. To keep the list readable, saves made by the same person within a short time are grouped into one item labelled **Edited · N saves**. Select that item to expand it. Select **Show every save** to show all of them, and **Hide saves** to collapse them again.

Areca does not remove history automatically. Revisions stay with the entry for as long as the entry exists, and deleted entries keep their history as well.

<Callout type="info" title="History is not a backup of the website">
Restoring a revision changes the draft only. Nothing changes on the live website until you mark the entry ready and go live.
</Callout>

## Restore an earlier version

Owners and agency admins can restore a revision. Editors can see the history, but cannot restore a revision.

![The History section in the Details panel of a Harbor & Pine Bakery post, listing saves and status changes, with a Restore button on a revision](/screenshots/entry-history.png)

<Steps>
<Step>

**Find the revision.** Open the entry, expand **Details**, and select the item you want in **History**. Use **Show every save** if you need an exact save.

</Step>
<Step>

**Choose Restore.** Press **Restore** on that revision.

</Step>
<Step>

**Confirm.** In the **Restore this revision?** dialog, read the note and press **Restore**. The dialog says the current draft is replaced with this revision, and that your current draft is kept as a revision, so you can go back.

</Step>
</Steps>

After the restore, the editor shows **Revision restored**. Check the content, then mark the entry ready and go live if it should be on the website.

If you restore the wrong revision, open history again and restore the revision you had before. Your previous draft is also in history.

## Activity log

The activity log records actions that change the site or the client. Read it to answer questions such as "who marked this page ready?" or "why did the last release fail?"

### Who can see the log

| Where | Who can see it |
| --- | --- |
| **Activity** for a site, from the sidebar or the Overview | Owners and agency admins. |
| **Agency › Activity**, for all clients or one client | Agency admins. |

Editors do not see the activity log. They can still see the history of entries they edit.

### What is recorded

Each entry in the log is a sentence describing the action, with the person who did it. Automatic actions show **System** or "Started automatically". The log includes:

- **Entries:** created, edited, marked ready, moved back to draft, scheduled, schedule cancelled, archived, deleted and restored to an earlier version.
- **Releases:** going live started, went live and failed to go live, and an earlier version of the site restored. A scheduled change that could not go live is also recorded.
- **Content types:** saved and deleted.
- **Site:** settings changed, site created or deleted, and a new site token made.
- **People:** invitations sent, sent again, accepted or cancelled, members added or removed, and roles changed.
- **Clients:** created, updated or deleted, and deployment connections changed. Agency admins also see access changes, branding changes and AI settings changes.

Use the **Show more** button at the bottom of the list to load older entries. On the agency **Activity** page, use **Show activity for** to show one client, or all clients.

![The site Activity log listing recent entry changes, releases and invitations, each with the person who did it, and a Show more button at the bottom](/screenshots/site-activity.png)

<Callout type="info" title="The log is for review">
Activity shows what happened. It does not show the content of each change. Use the entry's history to see what changed in the text.
</Callout>
