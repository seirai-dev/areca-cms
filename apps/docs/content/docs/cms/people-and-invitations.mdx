title: People and invitations
description: Invite people to a client, choose Owner or Editor, resend or revoke invitations, change roles, and remove people.
---

People get access to a **client**, not to individual sites. Everyone who is a member of a client can open all of that client's sites. You invite and manage people from the client's **People** list, and agency admins can also see everyone across the agency.

Owners and agency admins manage people. Editors don't see the **People** list.

![People list for a site, showing two members with Owner and Editor role toggles and an Invite people button](/screenshots/current-people.png)

## Where to find people

- **On a site:** select **People** in the site's sidebar. It shows the members of the site's client.
- **On a client:** open the client in the **Agency** workspace, then select the **People** tab.
- **Across the agency:** in the **Agency** workspace, select **People** to see every account, and who has agency access.

Both views list the members of the same client.

## Roles

Each member of a client has one of two roles. The role applies to every site of that client.

| Role | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything an editor can do, plus make changes live and manage people. |
| **Editor** | Write and edit drafts. |

Agency admins have agency-wide access in addition to these roles. For the full list of what each role can do, see [roles and permissions](/docs/security/roles-and-permissions).

## Invite people

<Steps>
<Step>

**Open People.** Select **Invite people**.

</Step>
<Step>

**Enter the email addresses.** Separate several addresses with commas. Areca sends one invitation to each.

</Step>
<Step>

**Choose the role.** Under **They'll join as**, select **Owner** or **Editor**.

</Step>
<Step>

**Select the send button.** It shows how many invitations it will send. Areca then reports the result for each address.

</Step>
</Steps>

![The Invite people sheet with email addresses entered, the Owner or Editor role choice, and a send button showing how many invitations it will send](/screenshots/invite-people.png)

Each invitation email contains a link to join the client. The link expires **7 days** after it is created.

If an email isn't delivered, Areca still keeps the invitation. It shows **Email not delivered** in the pending list, and you can select **Send again**. Email delivery depends on how the installation is set up. See [storage and email](/docs/self-hosting/storage-and-email).

Areca won't create a second invitation for an address that already has a pending one, or invite someone who is already a member. Use **Send again** for a pending invitation instead.

## Manage pending invitations

The **Invitations** section lists people who haven't accepted yet. Each row shows when the invitation expires, or **Expired** when the time has passed.

- **Copy link** copies the invitation link, so you can send it another way.
- **Send again** sends a new email with a fresh link and a new 7-day expiry. The previous link stops working.
- **Revoke** cancels the invitation. Its link stops working straight away. You can invite the same address again later.

## Change a member's role

In the **Members** list, select **Owner** or **Editor** on the person's row. The change takes effect straight away.

A client must always have at least one owner. Areca won't let you demote or remove the last owner, so if you are the last owner, add another owner before you change your own role.

## Remove a member

Select the **More actions** menu on the person's row, then **Remove from** the client's name. Confirm in the dialog.

The person loses access to the client and all of its sites. You can invite them again later.

## Agency admins

Agency admins are different from client owners. An agency admin can manage every client, site and person, and can change content types, deployments, branding and AI settings. To grant or remove agency access, open **Agency › People** and change the person's agency admin access from there.

Agency admins must use two-factor authentication, and some actions ask for a fresh authenticator code. See [two-factor and step-up](/docs/security/two-factor-and-step-up).
