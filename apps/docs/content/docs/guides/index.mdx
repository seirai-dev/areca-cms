In this guide you build a small website for a fictional bakery, **Harbor & Pine Bakery**. The site has a list of news posts and one page for each post. Editors write the posts in Areca. Your website shows the posts that are ready.

You do not need to know Areca yet. Each step explains the words it uses.

Prefer an agent-assisted setup? Follow [Developer setup with Areca skills](/docs/guides/skills-setup) for the manual prerequisites, installation and handoff.

## The big picture

Think of a restaurant.

- The **CMS** is the kitchen. Editors write the posts and check them.
- The **site SDK** is the waiter. It takes the posts that are approved and carries them to your website.
- Your **website** is the dining room. Visitors read the posts there.

A post starts as a **draft**. Visitors never see a draft. When an editor marks a draft as **ready**, it is approved, but still not public. When an owner presses **Go live**, Areca packs every approved post into a **release**. Your website is then built and deployed with those posts, and visitors see them.

## What you need

- **Node.js 22.6 or newer.** Node 24 is recommended. Check with `node --version`.
- **pnpm**, the tool that installs packages. Check with `pnpm --version`. If it is missing, run `npm install -g pnpm`.
- **A running Areca CMS.** Your agency may run one for you. To run your own on your computer, follow the [quickstart](/docs/quickstart).
- **An agency admin account** in the CMS. Only agency admins create clients and sites. Admins sign in with a code from an authenticator app.

## The steps

1. [Create the client and the site](/docs/guides/create-the-site) in the CMS.
2. [Start the project and install the SDK](/docs/guides/start-the-project) on your computer.
3. [Describe your content](/docs/guides/describe-your-content) in a config file.
4. [Send the content plan to the CMS](/docs/guides/push-the-schema).
5. [Write posts and go live](/docs/guides/write-and-go-live).
6. [Pull the published content](/docs/guides/pull-the-content) into your project.
7. [Show the posts on your website](/docs/guides/render-the-pages).
8. [Preview drafts](/docs/guides/preview-drafts) (optional).
9. [Deploy and publish for real](/docs/guides/deploy-and-next).

Plan for about an hour and a half. Steps 1 to 7 are the core. You can stop after step 7 and still have a working site.

<Callout title="Words you will see">
- **Client:** the business you build a website for.
- **Site:** one website for a client.
- **Collection:** a kind of content, such as posts. Areca calls it a content type in some screens.
- **Entry:** one item in a collection, such as one post.
- **Release:** a frozen set of approved content that goes live together.
</Callout>
